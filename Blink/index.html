About

Blink
Version 1.0 — MVP

Your workday in the blink of an eye. Blink brings together your calendar, Slack digest, Drive files, and work tools into a fast, browser-native dashboard — no installs, no friction.

What's working
📆 Google Calendar — Day, Week & Month views with on-call, OOO & recharge banners
📬 Gmail — unread count with inbox link
📁 Google Drive — shared, mentioned & created docs, sheets & slides (last 30 days)
💬 Slack Digest — daily summary of key channels with Region Handover banner
🚨 On-call schedule — last & next weekend coverage
🏖️ OOO & recharge days in Calendar banners
🗂️ Cases & Workday — configurable links to your org's tools
🎨 5 color themes + dark mode
⚡ Setup wizard for new users
💾 All data saved locally — no account needed
