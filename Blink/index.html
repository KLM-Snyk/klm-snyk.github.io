Blink
Version 19.66

Your workday in the blink of an eye. A single pane of glass for support managers — Google Suite, Slack, Jira, and your shift calendar all in one place. No installs, no context switching.

Key features
📆 Calendar — Day/Week/Month, on-call & OOO
📬 Mail — inbox unread count, plus a full 30-day searchable Mail screen
💬 Slack Digest — Region Handover + AI recap
🏢 Workday — notifications from Slack, grouped into pending/approved/updates
🎫 JIRA — your assigned issues, grouped by project
📊 Support Case Trends & Data — 7 charts (backlog, resolution time, submitted/solved, all split Support vs R&D), plus Salesforce quick links
📁 Google Drive — shared, mentions & created (last 30 days)
🔴 Snyk status banner — flags an active outage/incident, links to status.snyk.io
🎨 5 color themes + dark mode
⚡ Setup wizard — Google + Slack + Jira + Workday SSO
